## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
PaddockCore checks what crosses between a sandbox and an A2UI web tab, without the renderer, on Linux: A2UIInbox takes the tab's JSONL log line by line (v1.0, the playground catalog, 512 KB a message, 8 MB a tab; each refused line told to the agent), A2UIPolicy.lineForAgent shapes what the page sends back, A2UICommands builds every sbx exec (delivery on stdin), A2UIMedia resolves media to a sandbox fetch, a sandbox file, or data:, A2UILink classifies clicked links. The sandbox's paddock keeps a tab's messages as JSONL and serves --messages, --operator a2ui, --read, --fetch and --show-file; ProjectRunner has the matching methods. Ported from the native branch; 25 A2UI tests on the playground fixtures, 635 + 36 tests and lint pass.
<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:END -->

## Final Summary

<!-- SECTION:FINAL_SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
Info has a Running Agents card (ProjectAgentsCard): every agent session the sandbox reports with its mark, state, for how long, and where it is (LeftAgents.Place); Show for one in a tab, Take Over for one left running or in another Paddock's tab (asks first when it works or another Paddock shows it), Stop for every one in a Paddock tab with a known process (with a confirmation). Info has a Processes card (ProjectProcessesCard): the Processes tab's table without its header, with Stop and a button to open the tab. LeftAgents.others finds agents in another Paddock's tabs (tested); the sidebar lists them (place otherPaddock) next to left-running ones, and both rows open the project's Info. As the operator asked mid-way, there are no tabs for these agents: the left-running tabs of PAD-153.2 are removed (LeftAgentView, host tabs), and closing an agent or shell tab still stops its agents. System > Processes lists only sbx's processes on the Mac again. Doc UI and CHANGELOG. Not compiled on the Mac yet.
